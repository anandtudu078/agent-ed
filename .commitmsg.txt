feat: bilingual teaching in Hindi and English

Students can now choose the language the owl teaches in, and the choice is
stored on the user so it follows them to another device.

What changed
- User.language (en | hi), persisted in MongoDB, returned by register/login.
- PATCH /api/auth/language persists the preference and re-issues the token,
  so the language in the JWT never goes stale.
- Tutor prompts take a language clause; the Groq fallback was dropping it,
  which meant Hindi students silently got English whenever Gemini was out
  of quota - which, on the free tier, is most of the time.
- Diagram labels follow the same code-switching rule as the prose.
- The owl's own idle/thinking/speaking lines are localised.
- Noto Sans Devanagari added to the font stack.

Code-switching, not translation
Hindi teaching keeps technical terms in English ("recursion ek function hai
jo apne aap ko call karta hai"). Transliterating the vocabulary into
Devanagari would make it less recognisable, not more - and it keeps the text
readable on devices with no Hindi support.

Speech degrades honestly
Many machines have no hi-IN voice. Rather than read Devanagari through an
English voice, which sounds like gibberish, the client detects the missing
voice, says so, and falls back to a silent diagram walkthrough.

Verified: Hindi reply, code-switched diagram labels, 72/72 UI checks,
13/13 security checks, both TS projects compile.

Still open: assessments are graded on English-only prompts, and full UI
i18n (dashboard, courses) is deliberately deferred.
