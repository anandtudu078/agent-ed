What it actually does

🧠

It asks, it doesn't tell

Socratic mode asks one focused question at a time and builds on your last answer rather than repeating it. It withholds the answer on purpose — that is the entire method.

⏸️

It stops and waits

In Teach mode the explanation breaks into beats and pauses at a check point. The lesson holds until you reply instead of scrolling past you while you read.

📡

Works offline

With no provider key or no connection, the tutor still answers from a local fallback — on the server, so the refusal is real rather than hidden in the browser.

English and Hindi

The tutor teaches and asks questions in either language, switchable mid-lesson. Your answer may be a mix of the two — an Hinglish answer to an English question is graded as understanding, not as a spelling mistake.

Before you create an account

Every tutor prompt — your questions, your mistakes, your progress — is sent to a third-party AI provider to generate a reply. That is the core of what this product does, so it is stated here rather than buried in a policy page.

  • • We store your display name, username, a hashed password, your conversation history, and a record of what you have found easy and hard.
  • • Your messages go to Groq or Google Gemini to be answered. They are not used to train that provider's models on your account.
  • • You can export everything we hold about you, or delete the account completely, at any time — and the right to see your own data does not depend on having agreed to anything.

Anyone under 18 needs a named adult to agree on their behalf. That rule is enforced by the server, not just hidden behind a checkbox in the browser.

What this does not do yet

  • Guardian consent is self-reported. A guardian types their name; there is no email, verification step or follow-up, so it records an assertion rather than a confirmed identity.
  • No teacher or cohort view. A class cannot be granted access centrally, and a teacher cannot see what their students have asked. Access is per-account.
  • Deletion is immediate and unrecoverable. There is no backup to restore from and no grace period.

Read the same notice in the app — it is served from one source, so the two cannot drift apart.
