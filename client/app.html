    What this build does not do

    • Guardian consent is self-reported. A guardian types their name; there is no email, verification or follow-up step, so it records an assertion rather than a confirmed identity. Treat it accordingly.
    • Deletion is immediate and total but unrecoverable — there is no backup to restore from, and no grace period to change your mind.
    • Teacher accounts are self-declared. Ticking "I'm a teacher" at sign-up grants the class view with no approval step. A teacher sees a student's progress and weak topics only after that student joins the class with its code — never their conversations — but the role itself is only as trustworthy as the person holding the account.

    For teachers and parents

    Anyone under 18 needs a named adult to agree before the AI features unlock, and the server refuses them regardless of what the browser does. Students can export everything held about them or delete the account outright at any time — the right to see your own data does not depend on having agreed to anything.
